🧑‍🤝‍🧑 1. Personal Account

Persons, firms, companies. Example: Ram's A/c 👤

Debit the receiver, Credit the giver.

💸 Paid cash to Ram → Ram A/c Dr. (Ram received)

🏠 2. Real Account

Assets and properties. Example: Cash, Furniture 🪑

Debit what comes in, Credit what goes out.

🪑 Bought furniture for cash → Furniture A/c Dr. (comes in), Cash A/c Cr. (goes out)

💼 3. Nominal Account

Expenses, losses, incomes, gains. Example: Salary, Rent, Commission

Debit all expenses and losses, Credit all incomes and gains.

👷 Paid salary → Salary A/c Dr.

🏭 COGS = Opening Stock + Purchases − Closing Stock = 0

💹 Gross Profit = Sales − COGS = 0

🏁 Net Profit = Gross Profit + Other Income − Expenses = 0

If Net Profit is negative, it is a Net Loss 📉.

📒 Make a Journal entry in 4 steps

1️⃣ Find the two accounts in the transaction.
2️⃣ Decide the type of each (Personal / Real / Nominal).
3️⃣ Apply the rule to find which is Dr. and which is Cr.
4️⃣ Write Dr. first, then "To" + Cr. account (slightly to the right), the same amount on both sides, and a short narration in brackets.

⚡ Quick Dr./Cr. cheat sheet

SituationEntry
💵 Money comes inDr. Cash
💸 Money goes outCr. Cash
🧾 Expense paid (rent, salary)Dr. Expense
🎁 Income received (commission)Cr. Income
🏁 Owner starts businessCr. Capital
🪑 Asset boughtDr. Asset
🛒 Goods bought / soldDr. Purchases / Cr. Sales

🚫 Common mistakes

❌ Forgetting "To" before the credit account
❌ Dr. total not equal to Cr. total
❌ Mixing Purchases and Sales
❌ Putting Balance c/d on the wrong side
❌ Forgetting the narration

📚 Make a Ledger in 5 steps

1️⃣ Draw a T-shape for each account and write its name.
2️⃣ Copy from the journal: if the account is debited, write on the left "To" + the other account.
3️⃣ If it is credited, write on the right "By" + the other account.
4️⃣ Add both sides. The difference goes on the smaller side as Balance c/d.
5️⃣ Write both totals on the same line. Balance b/d starts the next period.
